# WEEK 3 - TWO POINTERS + SLIDING WINDOW

## Overall Goal

* Identify pattern instantly
* Move pointers correctly without confusion
* Convert brute force → O(n)

---

# DAY 1 - Two Pointers (Same Direction)

## Goal

Learn in-place array manipulation

## Concepts

* Two pointers moving forward
* Write pointer vs read pointer
* Maintaining order

## Problems

* Move Zeroes
* Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array
* Remove Element

## Focus

* Do NOT use extra space
* Understand pointer roles clearly

---

# DAY 2 - Two Pointers (Opposite Direction)

## Goal

Master left-right pointer strategy

## Concepts

* Left pointer (start), right pointer (end)
* Greedy movement logic

## Problems

* Container With Most Water
* Two Sum II (sorted array)
* Valid Palindrome

## Focus

* WHY pointer moves (not just how)
* Especially for container problem:
  → always move smaller height

---

# DAY 3 - Sliding Window (Core)

## Goal

Make window logic natural

## Concepts

* Expand window (right pointer)
* Shrink window (left pointer)
* Maintain valid condition

## Problems

* Longest Substring Without Repeating
* Maximum Average Subarray

## Focus

* No restarting window
* Always maintain valid state

---

# DAY 4 - Sliding Window (Variable Conditions)

## Goal

Handle dynamic constraints

## Concepts

* Condition-based shrinking
* Tracking counts inside window

## Problems

* Longest Repeating Character Replacement
* Subarray Product Less Than K

## Focus

* When to shrink
* How to maintain condition efficiently

---

# DAY 5 - Advanced Sliding Window

## Goal

Handle interview-level problems

## Concepts

* Expand → satisfy condition
* Shrink → optimize answer

## Problems

* Minimum Window Substring
* Longest Substring with At Most K Distinct Characters

## Focus

* Balance between:

  * valid window
  * minimum size

---

# DAY 6 - Mixed Pattern Practice

## Goal

Pattern recognition under pressure

## Concepts

* Switching between patterns quickly

## Problems (pick 4)

* Move Zeroes
* Container With Most Water
* Longest Substring
* Subarray Product < K
* Valid Palindrome
* Longest Repeating Replacement

## Focus

* Identify pattern in <30 seconds
